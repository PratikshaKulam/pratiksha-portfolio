SmartLoan Connect · UX/UI design at Mphasis

People started applying. Then they quietly gave up.

A digital lending platform I designed for a large private-sector bank in India. Customers were starting loan applications online but abandoning them before submitting — so I reworked the entire journey to reduce friction and build trust at every step.

The problem

The flow worked. People still left.

Context

Moving loan applications out of the branch.

The bank wanted customers to apply for personal and business loans online instead of visiting a branch. The flow technically worked — but a large share of applicants abandoned the process before submitting, and no one was sure exactly where or why they were dropping off.

The signals

Abandonment

High

Applications started online but never submitted.

Where

The income step

The single biggest drop-off point in the flow.

Side effect

↑ Support calls

Confused applicants phoning the bank mid-form.

02

Research & discovery

I dug into why before touching the design.

Inputs

Workshops, tickets, analytics — and every screen mapped.

I ran requirement workshops with business stakeholders, reviewed customer support tickets and existing application analytics, and mapped every screen from eligibility check to disbursement. Four clear patterns came out of it.

Insight 1

No sense of progress

Applicants had no idea how many steps were left, so a long form felt endless — and they quit partway through.

Insight 2

Confusing financial jargon

Terms written for bankers, not customers. People weren't sure what was being asked or whether they qualified.

Insight 3

The income step broke trust

The income & verification screen was the biggest drop-off — unclear fields, no guidance, felt risky to enter.

Insight 4

Document upload guesswork

Users didn't know which documents were valid, uploaded the wrong ones, and applications stalled in review.

04

Key design decisions

Four moves that removed the friction.

Traceable

Each decision maps directly to one of the research insights — I wanted every change to be traceable to a real reason, not a guess.

01

A persistent progress tracker

People always know where they are and how much is left — the endless-form feeling disappears.

→ Insight 1 · no sense of progress
02

Plain-language, chunked forms

Jargon rewritten in human terms; one long form broken into small, focused steps.

→ Insight 2 · financial jargon
03

Guided income step + reassurance

The riskiest-feeling step now explains why the information is needed and signals security — rebuilding trust.

→ Insight 3 · income step broke trust
04

Document upload with examples

Clear examples of valid documents and inline validation, so people upload the right thing first time.

→ Insight 4 · upload guesswork
