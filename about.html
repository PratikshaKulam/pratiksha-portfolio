The designer

I am the kind of designer who…

01

…reads the error state before the happy path.

Edge cases aren't edge cases to real users. I design for the moment things go wrong — because that's when trust is built or broken.

02

…interviews users before touching Figma.

70% of my time is in research. Finding the right problem is half the solution — the other half is making sure you've actually talked to the people who have it.

03

…speaks developer — because I used to be one.

CS degree + frontend experience means I design with implementation in mind and hand off without surprises. No redlines that can't actually be built.

04

…thinks AI should serve people, not impress them.

I design for LLM-integrated experiences and conversational UI — not because it's trendy, but because that's where the hardest UX problems are right now.

05

…treats accessibility as a starting point, not a checkbox.

WCAG compliance, screen reader logic, keyboard navigation — baked in from day one. If it doesn't work for everyone, it's not done.
