My story

I started in computer science — writing code, thinking in systems, solving problems with logic. Then I discovered that the most interesting problems weren't technical. They were human.

That shift led me to Human-Computer Interaction. Now I sit at the intersection of both worlds: I understand how the system works, and I advocate for the person using it. That combination is rare — and it's what makes my work different.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably testing a new restaurant in Chicago, doom-scrolling design Twitter, or pretending I'll finish that Duolingo streak.
