Project overview

Overview

Understanding autonomous delivery trust

This project explores how people perceive robot-assisted food delivery when the interface gives limited context and control.

The Problem

Trust drops when the handoff is unclear

Users can track drivers but struggle to understand how robot delivery works, especially around pickup moments and failure states.

Research

Mixed methods for behavior and sentiment

Research blended secondary analysis, competitor scans, moderated interviews, and flow heuristics to define the highest-friction moments.

Research Artifact Placeholder

Process

From current-state mapping to iterative flow redesign

The concept evolved from 3 low-fidelity screens into a 12-screen journey focused on confidence cues, progress visibility, and clearer recovery paths.

Process Flow Placeholder

Solution

A calmer, clearer delivery narrative

The redesign introduces transparent status updates, contextual microcopy, and key moments where users regain control without increasing cognitive load.

Outcomes / Impact

A stronger concept for product readiness

The final direction surfaces one meaningful product gap and outlines a practical framework teams can use to evaluate robot delivery rollouts.

Reflection

Designing emerging tech needs narrative clarity

New interaction models require confidence-building language and predictable states as much as visual polish.
